WHO WE ARE

Engineering excellence you can trust.

Saad Engineering is a privately owned Bangladeshi company specializing in power, automation, and industrial engineering solutions. Since our establishment, we have been dedicated to delivering high-quality technical support, consultancy, and maintenance services across the power and manufacturing industries.

We provide complete, end-to-end solutions — from identifying the problem to implementing the most efficient and cost-effective solution. Our experienced engineers and technicians diagnose, repair and optimize complex electrical and mechanical systems.
